WHERE IT BEGINS

Dance Is More Than Movement.

Rasika was created with a simple belief: every person has a story that can be expressed through movement.

Whether it is the discipline of classical dance, the freedom of contemporary movement, the energy of hip-hop or the joy of Bollywood, Rasika gives dancers a place to learn, explore and express themselves.

We believe that dance is not only about learning steps. It is about discovering confidence, creativity, discipline and the courage to step onto the stage.
